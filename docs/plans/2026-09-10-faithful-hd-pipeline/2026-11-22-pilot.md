# HD pilot: encyclopedia pictures, 2026-11-22

The user asked for a pilot and a choice of style only; the scope of HD work
stays an open decision.

## Samples

Five EDATA pictures (400 by 200, 8-bit indexed, 187 in all): EDATA.074 (a
portrait), .062 (a Star Destroyer), .007 (a facility), .153 (Vader before
the Empire's emblem), .170 (a planet). The source and 4x outputs are kept
outside Git in `.artifacts/hd-pilot-2026-11-22/` (`src/`, `out/<method>/`,
`compare-NNN.png` crops at 4x).

## Classical 4x methods (done)

| Method | Result on this art |
|---|---|
| nearest | Exact; the 256-colour dithering shows as coarse noise |
| Lanczos | Smooth, closest to the intended picture, but soft, not sharper |
| hqx (ffmpeg) | Keeps the dithering, smooths only hard edges; little gain |
| xBR (ffmpeg) | Turns the dithering into blobs ("oil painting"); worse than the source |

The encyclopedia art is painted or rendered imagery dithered into a 256-colour
palette, not pixel art. Pixel-art scalers (hqx, xBR, Scale2x) are the wrong
family for it. A real gain needs a model that removes the dithering and
reconstructs detail: Real-ESRGAN x4plus, 4x-FSDedither, UltraSharp V2, SwinIR
or HAT (the plan's candidates for painted art).

`realesrgan-x4plus-anime`. A PyTorch venv with safetensors checkpoints
(UltraSharp V2, FSDedither) would be the next step.

## Findings so far

- Interface chrome, text, buttons and small sprites: nearest 4x (the plan's
  rule) — any smoothing changes glyphs and silhouettes.
- Painted pictures (encyclopedia, portraits, planets, ships): only an
  AI dither-removing model can make them look sharper; without one, Lanczos
  is the most faithful choice and gives no real HD effect.
- The renderer draws bitmaps scaled straight to the window (no 640x480
  intermediate target), so HD pictures would show their detail on large
  screens.
- Open decisions: scope (pilot / interface / everything), how far a model
  may invent detail (faithful vs remaster profile), an in-game switch, and
  the HD pack's hosting (built locally, never in Git).
