# HD pilot: encyclopedia pictures, 2026-11-22

The user asked for a pilot and a choice of style only; the scope of HD work
stays an open decision.

## Samples

Five EDATA pictures (400 by 200, 8-bit indexed, 187 in all): EDATA.074 (a
portrait), .062 (a Star Destroyer), .007 (a facility), .153 (Vader before
the Empire's emblem), .170 (a planet). The source and 4x outputs are kept
outside Git in `.artifacts/hd-pilot-2026-11-22/` (`src/`, `out/<method>/`,
`compare-NNN.png` crops at 4x).

## Classical 4x methods (done)

| Method | Result on this art |
|---|---|
| nearest | Exact; the 256-colour dithering shows as coarse noise |
| Lanczos | Smooth, closest to the intended picture, but soft, not sharper |
| hqx (ffmpeg) | Keeps the dithering, smooths only hard edges; little gain |
| xBR (ffmpeg) | Turns the dithering into blobs ("oil painting"); worse than the source |

The encyclopedia art is painted or rendered imagery dithered into a 256-colour
palette, not pixel art. Pixel-art scalers (hqx, xBR, Scale2x) are the wrong
family for it. A real gain needs a model that removes the dithering and
reconstructs detail: Real-ESRGAN x4plus, 4x-FSDedither, UltraSharp V2, SwinIR
or HAT (the plan's candidates for painted art).

## AI methods (run with the user's approval)

Real-ESRGAN ncnn-vulkan, official release v0.2.5.0
(`realesrgan-ncnn-vulkan-20220424-ubuntu.zip`, sha256
`e5aa6eb131234b87c0c51f82b89390f5e3e642b7b70f2b9bbe95b6a285a40c96`, in
`~/tools/realesrgan-ncnn/`), on the GPU, about a second per picture.
The style sheets `style-074.png`, `style-062.png`, `style-007.png` show four
styles side by side:

| Style | Result |
|---|---|
| A: Lanczos | Faithful, soft; keeps the dithering as a fine grain |
| B: AI `realesrgan-x4plus` | Dithering gone, sharp edges and clean gradients (Vader's backdrop); fine textures smoothed: the Star Destroyer's hull plating thins out, the face looks airbrushed, the mountains lose grain |
| C: 70% B + 30% A | Sharp shapes with some of the original's texture back; the most "1998 art, only sharper" look |
| D: AI `realesrgan-x4plus-anime` | Painted, cartoon-like; invents lines (Luke's face); too far from the original |

D is out. B and C are the candidates; C needs no new model, only a fixed
blend over B, so it stays deterministic. UltraSharp V2 and FSDedither
(PyTorch, safetensors) were not tried.

## Findings so far

- Interface chrome, text, buttons and small sprites: nearest 4x (the plan's
  rule) — any smoothing changes glyphs and silhouettes.
- Painted pictures (encyclopedia, portraits, planets, ships): only an
  AI dither-removing model can make them look sharper; without one, Lanczos
  is the most faithful choice and gives no real HD effect.
- The renderer draws bitmaps scaled straight to the window (no 640x480
  intermediate target), so HD pictures would show their detail on large
  screens.
- Open decisions: scope (pilot / interface / everything), how far a model
  may invent detail (faithful vs remaster profile), an in-game switch, and
  the HD pack's hosting (built locally, never in Git).

## More methods (2026-11-24, with the user's approval to install PyTorch)

PyTorch 2.15.0+cu132 with `spandrel` 0.4.2 in `~/tools/hd-torch` (a venv,
5.4 GB), run on the RTX 3060; models in `~/tools/hd-models`, each checked

| 4x-FSDedither (ESRGAN) | OpenModelDB (Google Drive), GPL-3.0 | `be2e991e…dff133` |

Sheets: `methods-{074,062,007}.png` (B, C and the four models) and
`combos-{074,062,007}.png` (C, UltraSharpV2, SwinIR-L, and two chains:
FSDedither 4x, Lanczos back to 400x200, then UltraSharpV2 or SwinIR-L).
Time for the five pictures: FSDedither 3 s, UltraSharpV2 11 s, SwinIR-L
12 s, HAT-L 24 s.

| Method | Result |
|---|---|
| E: UltraSharpV2 | The most detail (hull greebles, wall texture), but the 256-colour dithering comes back as fine speckle on flat surfaces and the face gains lines the picture never had |
| F: FSDedither | Removes the dithering; soft, some blocky patches |
| G: SwinIR-L | Clean, close to B, a little more natural |
| H: HAT-L (Nomos8k) | Treats the dithering as texture: grainier than E |
| I: FSDedither -> UltraSharpV2 | Best on the face and the Star Destroyer: clean, sharp, plate texture and detail kept, no speckle, no invented lines; flattens the mountains' shading into patches on the facility |
| J: FSDedither -> SwinIR-L | Clean but waxy, over-strong blue edges |

So far: C stays the safe all-round style; I is the strongest on portraits
and ships and worth a wider test (all 187 pictures, landscapes in
particular). UltraSharpV2's licence is non-commercial; the HD pack is built
locally and never committed, so that matters only if packs were ever
shared. The choice stays the user's.
